RCSB notes that PDB archive data is CC0 and that structure images should cite
the PDB entry and RCSB PDB where possible. PDBe PDBImages renders are generated
from the same PDB archive structures.

Wikimedia assets are used as textbook-style supporting figures. Prefer local
copies under `static/courses/protein-folding/` when Wikimedia allows download;
otherwise keep the Commons source page in this table and use the canonical
upload URL in Markdown.
