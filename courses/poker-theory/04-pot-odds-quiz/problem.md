# Pot Odds Quiz

This quiz drills the pot-odds calculation and the calling decision from Module 03. You will see a mix of conceptual questions and live calculation drills where the pot and bet sizes are randomly generated on every attempt.

**What's covered:**

1. Computing pot odds as a percentage: $\text{call} \div (\text{pot} + \text{bet} + \text{call})$
2. Finding break-even equity from pot odds
3. Applying the calling equation: call if equity ≥ pot odds
4. Converting outs to equity with the Rule of 2 and 4, then comparing to pot odds
5. Conceptual questions about implied odds and when to deviate from pure pot odds

**How to approach the calculation questions:**

For any bet scenario:
1. Write down the call amount.
2. Add pot + bet + call to find the total pot after calling.
3. Divide call by total pot and multiply by 100.

The numbers are designed to be mental-math friendly — multiples of common bet sizes. With practice, this should take under 5 seconds per question.

Good luck!
