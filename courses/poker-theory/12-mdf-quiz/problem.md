# MDF & Alpha Quiz

This quiz drills the three core formulas from module 11:

- **MDF** = pot / (pot + bet) — the minimum fraction of your range you must continue with
- **Alpha (α)** = bet / (pot + bet) — the break-even fold frequency; the maximum fraction you can fold; also the bluff-to-value *ratio* (bluffs per value combo)
- **Bluff fraction** = bet / (pot + 2·bet) — the share of bluffs in a balanced betting range (this equals the pot odds you lay the caller, and is *smaller* than alpha)

Each parametric question generates fresh numbers on every attempt. Work through each calculation before checking your answer — the goal is speed and accuracy, not pattern recognition.

**Tip:** For pot-sized bets, MDF = 50% exactly. For half-pot bets, MDF = 67%. Use these anchors to sanity-check your arithmetic.
