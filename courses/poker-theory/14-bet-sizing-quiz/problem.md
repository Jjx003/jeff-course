# Bet Sizing Quiz

This quiz tests your ability to apply the two core formulas from module 13:

- **Geometric sizing:** $g = \text{SPR}^{1/n}$, then bet $(g-1)/2 \times$ current pot
- **MDF at a given bet size:** MDF = pot / (pot + bet), reappearing here in the context of choosing how large to bet

Parametric questions generate fresh numbers every attempt — work through the arithmetic rather than memorising specific answers. Focus on the *structure* of each formula so you can apply it to any stack depth and street count at the table.

**Reference anchors:**
- SPR 8, 3 streets → $g = 2.0$ → bet 50% pot each street
- SPR 9, 2 streets → $g = 3.0$ → bet pot-sized each street
- SPR 4, 2 streets → $g = 2.0$ → bet 50% pot each street
