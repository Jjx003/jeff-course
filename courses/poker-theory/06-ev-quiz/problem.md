# Expected Value Quiz

This quiz tests your ability to compute and interpret expected value for the three core poker decisions: calling, bluffing, and value betting.

**What's covered:**

1. The general EV formula: $\text{EV} = \sum P_i V_i$
2. EV of a call given equity, pot size, and call amount
3. EV of a bluff given fold frequency, pot size, and bet size
4. Break-even fold frequency: $f^* = B / (P + B)$
5. Conceptual questions about when bluffs and value bets are profitable

**Calculation drills** use randomised numbers — pot sizes, bet sizes, fold percentages, and equity percentages vary on every attempt. Write out the arithmetic; these are designed to be mental-math friendly with numbers that divide cleanly.

**One rounding note:** EV results are rounded to the nearest whole dollar (or nearest tenth where specified). Use the formula as written and round at the end.

Good luck — after this quiz, every bet you face will come with a number attached.
