# ICM Quiz

This quiz tests your understanding of the Independent Chip Model and tournament-specific decision-making.

**What's covered:**

1. The core ICM principle: chips you risk are worth more in dollar terms than chips you gain
2. When chip EV and dollar EV diverge — and why
3. Break-even call equity: $\text{equity} = \text{call} / \text{total pot}$
4. ICM-adjusted break-even thresholds for callers near the bubble
5. Push-fold logic: why short stacks push wide and callers need extra equity
6. Final table dynamics: big stack exploitation, pay jump awareness, short stack tightening

**Calculation drills** use randomised numbers — call sizes, pot sizes, and ICM penalties vary on every attempt. The arithmetic is designed to work out to clean whole-number percentages.

**One key distinction to keep in mind:** the break-even equity formula $\text{call}/\text{pot}$ gives the *chip-EV* break-even — the floor. ICM adjustments always raise this threshold for callers in real tournament spots. Knowing the chip-EV baseline, plus when and how much to add for ICM, is the core skill this quiz builds.

Good luck — after this quiz, every tournament all-in will come with two numbers: the chip-EV break-even and the ICM adjustment on top of it.
