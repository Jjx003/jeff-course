# GTO Quiz

You've studied Nash equilibrium, the indifference principle, and the GTO/exploitative trade-off. Now test whether the concepts have landed — conceptually and numerically.

The parametric questions drill the two core GTO formulas from Module 09:

$$\text{Bluff frequency} = \frac{B}{P + B} \qquad \text{Value-to-bluff ratio} = \frac{P}{B}$$

Each retake generates fresh numbers. Aim for 80%+ before proceeding to Module 11 (Minimum Defense Frequency), which extends these formulas to the defender's perspective.

**Topics covered:**

- What GTO guarantees (and what it doesn't)
- Mixed strategies and randomisation
- The indifference principle in practice
- GTO vs. exploitative play trade-offs
- Bluff frequency arithmetic
- Value-to-bluff ratio at different bet sizes
