# Combinatorics Quiz

Welcome to your first calculation drill. This quiz tests the combo-counting and out-estimation skills from Module 01.

You will encounter two kinds of questions:

- **Conceptual questions** — multiple choice and true/false that check your understanding of *why* the formulas work, not just whether you can plug numbers in.
- **Calculation drills** — parametric questions that generate fresh numbers on every attempt. No two runs of the quiz will look exactly the same, so each attempt is genuine practice, not memorisation.

**What's covered:**

1. How many combos a pocket pair has ($\binom{4}{2} = 6$) and why offsuit hands have 12
2. The total number of starting-hand combos ($\binom{52}{2} = 1{,}326$)
3. Which hands are suited vs. offsuit, and how combo counts change
4. Common out counts for standard drawing hands
5. Applying the Rule of 2 and Rule of 4 to convert outs → approximate equity

Take your time on the calculation drills — write the arithmetic out if it helps. The goal is to reach the point where you can run these numbers in your head at the table in under 5 seconds.

Good luck!
