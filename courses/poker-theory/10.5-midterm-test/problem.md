# Poker Theory Midterm

This midterm checks the first half of the course: probability foundations, pot odds, expected value, range thinking, and game-theory basics.

Work from principles rather than memory. You may want scratch paper for combo counts, pot-odds percentages, and EV comparisons.

The test mixes conceptual questions with calculation-style questions. Explanations and correctness are shown only after you submit the full attempt.

## Scope

- Combo counting, outs, and the Rule of 2 and 4
- Pot odds and break-even equity
- Expected value for calls, bluffs, and value bets
- Range advantage, nut advantage, blockers, and board interaction
- GTO basics, mixed strategies, indifference, and exploitative deviations
