# Pot Odds Speed Drill

This is a replayable fluency drill. The goal is not to memorize one answer; it is to get faster at turning a pot, a bet, and a call into the percentage you need.

Type the nearest whole-number percentage. The drill gives immediate feedback, tracks accuracy and average response time, and saves your personal best after each round.

Aim for accuracy first. Speed becomes meaningful once you can stay near the target accuracy.
