# Deck: Rapid Fire

Forty-two cards, drawn from the whole course, in the form the rapid-fire technical discussion actually uses.

This deck is different from the others. The earlier decks teach; this one **rehearses delivery**. Every answer here is short enough to say in under thirty seconds, because that is the budget you get before the interviewer moves on.

## How to use it

**Answer out loud, at conversational speed, in one breath.** If you find yourself constructing the answer while speaking, grade it *Again* — that hesitation is exactly what the format penalizes.

**Do not add.** These answers are already the right length. Rehearsing a two-minute version of a thirty-second answer trains the wrong thing: in a rapid-fire round, over-answering costs you the questions you would have got right.

**Answer the question that was asked, then stop.** Interviewers use silence to see if you will fill it. Filling it with hedging turns a good answer into an uncertain one.

## A note on not knowing

You will hit questions you cannot answer. "I do not know that one" costs you almost nothing when the other twelve answers were crisp. Bluffing costs you a great deal, because the follow-up will catch it and now the interviewer is discounting everything else you said.

Practice saying it. It is a skill.
