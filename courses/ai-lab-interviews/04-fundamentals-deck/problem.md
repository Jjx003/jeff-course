# Deck: Fundamentals

Forty cards covering everything in the fundamentals reading that a rapid-fire technical discussion can ask you to produce in under thirty seconds.

## How to use this deck

Work through it once now. After that, stop coming back to this page — the [Review](/review) queue will surface these cards along with every other deck in the course, on a schedule that keeps them loaded without you having to decide what to study.

**Grade honestly.** *Again* means you could not have said it out loud in an interview. Not "I recognized it" — recognition is not recall, and the interview tests recall. A deck full of dishonest *Good* grades is a deck that stops showing you the things you do not know.

**Say the answer out loud before you flip.** Silent reading gives you the fluency illusion. The interview is a speaking test.
