# Deck: Post-Training and RL

Twenty-eight cards on modules 29 and 30.

This is the area that changes fastest, so a card that is a year old can be stale. Two habits worth keeping while you use this deck:

- **Answer every method card with what it removed.** DPO removed the reward model and the sampling loop. GRPO removed the value network. That framing is what makes the family tree memorable and what makes your answer sound like understanding rather than recall.
- **Volunteer the cost.** Every one of these methods bought its simplification with something. Naming the trade unprompted is the difference between a good answer and a great one.
