# Final: Mock Technical Screen

Thirty-five questions drawn from every unit of the course, in the format of a rapid-fire technical discussion.

**This is a test, not a quiz.** You will not see whether you were right until the end. That is deliberate: in a real screen you get no feedback either, and you have to manage your own uncertainty for an hour without recalibrating after each answer.

## How to take it

**Say the full answer out loud before selecting.** The options are there to score you, not to help you. If you can only recognize the answer and not produce it, you have not learned the material — and in the actual interview there are no options.

**Do not look anything up.** The point is a calibrated measurement of where you are, and a measurement you helped is not a measurement.

**Note the ones you were unsure about even when you got them right.** Those are more informative than the ones you got wrong, because a guess that lands still leaves the gap.

## After

The result matters far less than the pattern. Sort your misses by unit:

| Missed in | Go back to |
|---|---|
| Fundamentals, backprop | modules 2–5 |
| Attention, position, norms | modules 6–12 |
| Accounting, memory, FLOPs | modules 13–14 |
| Optimizers, schedules, precision | modules 15–20 |
| Scaling, parallelism | modules 21–24 |
| Inference, serving, sampling | modules 25–28 |
| Post-training, RL | modules 29–31 |
| Tokenization, data, evaluation | modules 32–34 |
| Math | modules 35–38 |

Then keep the [Review](/review) queue running daily. Retake this in two weeks; the second score tells you whether the spaced repetition is working, which is the only thing you actually need to know at that point.

## A calibration note

If you clear 80% here, you are in reasonable shape for a breadth interview. That is not the same as being ready for an ML coding interview — those are tested by modules 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 16, 18, 26, 30, 33, and 40, with the assistant off and a timer running, and no multiple-choice test can substitute for that.

Good luck. It is a genuinely demanding process, and being prepared for it is the part you control.
