# Deck: Training, Accounting, and Precision

Fifty-one cards covering modules 13 through 18. This is the densest deck in the course in terms of interview value per card, because it is where the *numbers* live — and numbers are what turn a plausible answer into a convincing one.

A large fraction of these cards ask you to produce an arithmetic result. Do the arithmetic out loud, out to the answer, every time. Recognizing "16 bytes per parameter" is not the same skill as saying "16 bytes times 7 billion is 112 gigabytes, so no, not on one 80 gig card" in an interview at conversational speed.
