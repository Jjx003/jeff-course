# Deck: Inference and Serving

Thirty-six cards on modules 25 through 27.

Every card in this deck traces back to one sentence: **decoding is memory-bandwidth bound**. When you are grading yourself, check whether your answer connects to that. "Continuous batching improves throughput" is a fact. "Continuous batching improves throughput because decode is bandwidth-bound, so an idle slot is wasted bandwidth, and static batching idles most slots waiting on the longest request" is an answer.
