# Deck: Tokenization, Data, and Evaluation

Twenty-four cards on modules 32 and 33.

This deck is disproportionately useful in **experiment-design** interviews rather than rapid-fire ones. When you are asked "how would you find out whether X helps", the answer almost always involves data curation and evaluation methodology, and the candidates who do well are the ones who reach for contamination checks and prompt-format sensitivity without being prompted.
