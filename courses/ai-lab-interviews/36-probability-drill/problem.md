# Drill: Probability

The formulas from module 35 under a clock.

Math interviews are the format where hesitation costs the most, because there is nothing to type while you think. The point of this drill is to make the distribution facts and the standard results automatic, so that in the room your attention goes entirely to *which technique applies* rather than to recalling a variance formula.

## Conventions

- All answers are whole numbers. Percentages are rounded to the nearest integer.
- Some items ask for a value times 10 or times 100, so a fractional answer becomes an integer.
- Standard results assumed: $\mathbb{E}[\text{coupon collector}] = nH_n$, fair gambler's ruin from $i$ to $N$ is $i/N$, expected flips until HH is 6 and until HT is 4.

## Target

85% at 20 prompts in 150 seconds.
