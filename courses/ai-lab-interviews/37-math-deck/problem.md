# Deck: Math

Twenty-seven cards on module 35.

Unlike the other decks in this course, several of these are pure recall of a formula — and that is appropriate here. In a math interview there is nothing to type while you think, so a two-second delay recalling the variance of a geometric is a two-second delay the interviewer watches. Get these to the point where they arrive without effort, and spend your thinking on which technique applies.

The technique-selection cards are the highest-value ones in the deck. Those are the difference between "I know the geometric distribution" and "the word *memoryless* in that question means geometric".
