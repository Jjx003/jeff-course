# Tumor-board checklist

1. Is the target present, clonal, processed, and presented?
2. Are relevant T cells present and in contact with malignant cells?
3. Is dysfunction reversible, or is the clone absent/terminal?
4. Is the sample from baseline, on treatment, or progression?
5. Does the proposed combination repair the observed bottleneck?

## Common traps

- predictive versus merely prognostic biomarkers;
- bulk RNA used as proof of cellular contact;
- response rate used as a substitute for durable benefit;
- a plausible combination tested without target-engagement biopsies;
- calling every nonresponse "exhaustion."

For deep reading, compare a positive and negative combination trial. Draw each
biological premise, then mark whether the trial actually measured it.
