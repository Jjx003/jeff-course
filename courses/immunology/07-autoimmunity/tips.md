# Case-room checklist: autoimmunity

Start with a timeline, not a disease name: susceptibility, candidate trigger,
first immune abnormality, first tissue injury, amplification, and treatment.

- Label every biomarker **upstream**, **mediator**, **downstream**, **predictive**, or **unknown**.
- Do not infer mimicry from sequence similarity or one cross-reactive serum sample.
- Ask whether blood reflects the target tissue or merely what can leave it.
- Separate reversible inflammation from accumulated structural damage.
- Treat drug response as a perturbation of one node, not proof of the entire causal chain.

High-yield comparison: B-cell depletion can help by removing antigen presentation
and cytokine organization even when a disease is not transferable by serum antibody.
