# Study guide

## Retrieval prompts

- What exact pair does a conventional TCR recognize?
- Why can peptide pulsing localize a processing defect?
- What is the difference between a cell that primes and a cell that is killed?
- Why does normal antigen sequence not rule out presentation escape?

## Draw both routes

For MHC I and II, draw source compartment, degradation compartment, loading
site, presenting cell, and responding coreceptor. Then add cross-presentation as
an exception with a purpose, not as a third unrelated pathway.

## Common traps

- MHC I is not limited to immune cells.
- MHC II does not present only "bacterial" antigen; source and routing matter.
- TCR affinity alone does not determine response magnitude.
- Failure to kill does not prove failure to recognize.

## Interpret before naming

Recreate the four-target restriction table from memory. State the interaction
term in plain language before using the phrase "MHC restricted."
