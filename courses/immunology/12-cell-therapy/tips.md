# Product review card

For every construct, list: desired target cells, essential collateral cells,
antigen-density distributions, escape route, manufacturing bottleneck, acute
rescue, and late follow-up.

## Do not collapse these endpoints

- infused cells versus CAR-positive viable cells;
- expansion versus functional persistence;
- B-cell aplasia versus clinical remission;
- remission versus immune tolerance;
- switch activation versus successful rescue.

Audit one report by reconstructing the denominator: screened, collected,
manufactured, infused, evaluable, and followed long term. Manufacturing failures
and pre-infusion deterioration belong in the product assessment.
