# Deeper theory: confidence belongs to the claimed geometry

A global confidence average can be high while the interface depends on one
uncertain loop. This exercise therefore propagates residue-level uncertainty to
each contact: both sides must pass the threshold before the contact contributes to
hotspot ranking.

The rule is deliberately conservative and simplified. Real complex assessment can
also use pairwise alignment error, interface-specific confidence, model agreement,
templates, solvent accessibility, side-chain chemistry, and experimental context.
The transferable habit is to score confidence where the claim lives.

## Why contact count is only a prioritizer

A residue with several geometric contacts may be energetically unimportant, while
one salt bridge or buried hydrophobic contact may dominate binding. Mutation can
also perturb local or global folding. Contact count is useful for choosing a small
first panel, not for estimating binding free energy.

## Why uncertainty should remain visible

Discarding uncertain contacts entirely can hide a real flexible epitope. Treat them
as a separate experimental category. A structure or binding assay that resolves
the region can promote or reject the hypothesis without letting uncertain geometry
silently drive the main conclusion.
