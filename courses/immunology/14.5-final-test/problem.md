# Immunology final test

The final moves between molecules, cells, patients, and datasets. Keep the
level of inference explicit.

First reconstruct recognition, context, compartment, time, and control. Then
decide what level of claim the evidence supports: description, prediction,
mechanism, or decision utility.

| Observation supports | Observation alone does not prove |
|---|---|
| Association in a held-out cohort | Causal mechanism or clinical utility |
| Target engagement | Completion of every downstream immune step |
| A changed cell fraction | A changed absolute cell count |
| Many measured cells | Many independent patients |
| Early depletion | Durable immune reset |

Use the shortest causal chain that explains all observations. Prefer an answer
that names the remaining bottleneck, respects the experimental unit, and states
what a result can actually establish.
