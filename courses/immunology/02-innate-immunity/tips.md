# Study guide

## Retrieval prompts

- Why is cytosolic DNA more informative than DNA alone?
- What shared reaction connects all complement initiation routes?
- How could a normal neutrophil count coexist with poor bacterial clearance?
- What extra evidence would turn an inflammatory association into a causal claim?

## Three-column drill

For each pathway, write **trigger/location**, **immediate biochemical output**,
and **organism-level consequence**. This prevents skipping from receptor name
directly to disease.

## Common traps

- Complement is not synonymous with membrane attack complex.
- "Macrophage activation" is not one binary state.
- Blood cytokine concentration may not represent the affected tissue.
- Blocking an inflammatory pathway can reduce damage and impair host defense at
  the same time.

## Extend the plasma experiment

Design one follow-up that distinguishes failure to make C3 from accelerated C3
consumption. State the sample, measurement, and predicted result under each
hypothesis.
