# Capstone rubric

| Dimension | Strong work |
|---|---|
| scope | one population, decision, compartment, and time window |
| mechanism | competing explanations and intervention point are explicit |
| evidence | three primary studies compared by design and causal strength |
| numbers | at least one effect size or worked quantity is interpreted |
| experiment | feasible control, readouts, timing, and falsifier |
| figure | data and inference use distinct visual grammar |
| access | burden, cost, sampling, and safety affect the design |

## Final audit

Circle every use of "causes," "predicts," "restores," "durable," and
"personalized." For each, point to the evidence and time horizon. Replace any
unsupported claim with the narrower statement the study actually establishes.

Record the literature and registry search date. Current reviews map a field;
central claims still require the pivotal primary evidence.
