# Decision guide

When reading a vaccine claim, write down five nouns: **population, pathogen,
endpoint, tissue, time**. If one is missing, the claim is probably too broad.

## Common traps

- "90% effective" without an endpoint, denominator, or follow-up window;
- equating serum binding antibody with mucosal sterilizing immunity;
- treating reactogenicity as either proof of efficacy or proof of danger;
- calling a retrospective cutoff a validated correlate;
- using the homogeneous herd-immunity equation as a forecast.

## Practice

Take one licensed vaccine label or pivotal trial and make a one-page evidence
chain: product composition -> immune assay -> clinical endpoint -> known failure
mode -> post-licensure question. Calculate both relative and absolute effects.
