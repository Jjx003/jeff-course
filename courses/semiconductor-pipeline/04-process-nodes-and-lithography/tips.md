## Reading node comparisons

Do not compare "5 nm" at one foundry to "5 nm" at another as if both labels
were literal ruler measurements. Ask about density, performance at a given
power, power at a given performance, SRAM scaling, cost per transistor, yield,
and availability.

When a headline mentions EUV, ask which layers use EUV and how many exposures
are required. More EUV is not automatically better if cost, throughput, or
yield suffer.

When someone says a chip is "reticle-limited," they mean a single die is near
the scanner's maximum exposure field. That pushes designers toward chiplets or
careful floorplanning.
