# Hints and Going Deeper

## Reading hints

- When you see an accelerator announcement, separate **chip performance** from **system availability**. The first can be impressive while the second is limited by HBM, packaging, networking, or deployment.
- Treat HBM as both memory and packaging demand. It is not just another DRAM part on a board.
- Ask whether a bottleneck affects manufacturing output, rack integration, or actual cluster utilization. Those are different constraints.

## Questions to test yourself

1. Why can advanced packaging be a bottleneck even when leading-edge wafers are available?
2. Why does a custom ASIC still depend on the broader semiconductor ecosystem?
3. How do optics and switch silicon become part of an AI accelerator forecast?

## Going deeper

- Read vendor system diagrams, not only chip datasheets.
- Compare a GPU server BOM with a TPU pod or hyperscaler custom accelerator deployment.
- Look for analyst notes that separate HBM supply, CoWoS-like packaging, and data-center power availability.
