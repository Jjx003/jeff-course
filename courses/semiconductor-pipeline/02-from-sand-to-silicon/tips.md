## How to remember the flow

Think of the wafer as a layered map. Deposition adds land, lithography draws a
temporary stencil, etch cuts selected regions, implant changes electrical
properties, CMP flattens the terrain, and metrology checks whether the map still
matches the plan.

Do not memorize every tool acronym yet. First learn the verbs: add, expose,
remove, dope, polish, measure, test.

## Common pitfalls

Lithography is not the whole fab. It is one central step inside a repeated loop.

Packaging is not just a plastic shell. For leading AI chips, advanced packaging
can be a performance feature and a capacity bottleneck.

Wafer starts are not the same as good finished chips. Yield, die size, cycle
time, and packaging all sit between a wafer start and sellable product.
