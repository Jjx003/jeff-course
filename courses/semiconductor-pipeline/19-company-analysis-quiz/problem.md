# Company analysis capstone

This quiz checks whether you can use the course's analysis style on real
companies. The goal is not trivia. The goal is to map evidence to bottlenecks,
economic capture, and disconfirming signals.

Use the same frame:

- What scarce capability does the company control?
- Where is the current bottleneck?
- Who captures the economics?
- What evidence would change the thesis?
- What time lag matters?
