## Foundry economics check

This quiz tests the operating vocabulary behind the semiconductor pipeline:
who owns the design, who owns the factory, who packages the die, and why a few
percentage points of utilization or yield can matter more than a splashy product
announcement.

Treat each question like an investor or operator would. Ask:

- Which part of the chain is being paid for scarce capacity?
- Is the constraint wafers, good die, packaging slots, or customer demand?
- Does the business carry fixed costs that need high utilization?
- Is a yield change affecting revenue, cost per good die, or both?

Passing this quiz means you can read a foundry earnings call or AI accelerator
supply-chain note without getting lost in the nouns.
