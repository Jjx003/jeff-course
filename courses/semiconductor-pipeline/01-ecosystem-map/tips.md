## Hints for reading semiconductor news

Start by separating design, wafer fabrication, packaging, memory, and system
assembly. Many headlines blur these layers, but the economics and constraints
are different.

When someone says "capacity," ask capacity for what: wafers per month, EUV
scanner hours, CoWoS-like package starts, HBM stacks, substrates, test handlers,
or finished AI servers.

When someone says "supply chain," ask whether the issue is physical scarcity,
qualification time, export controls, capital spending, yield, customer
allocation, or simply price.
