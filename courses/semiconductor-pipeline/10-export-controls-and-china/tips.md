# Hints and Going Deeper

## Reading hints

- Keep policy analysis concrete. Identify the controlled capability, the affected layer, and the likely substitution path.
- Avoid treating "China semiconductors" as one market. Mature-node logic, DRAM, NAND, packaging, EDA, equipment, and AI accelerators have different bottlenecks.
- Separate **can build something** from **can build it at competitive yield, cost, power, and scale**.

## Questions to test yourself

1. Why do performance thresholds encourage product redesign?
2. Why can DUV restrictions matter even if EUV is the more famous bottleneck?
3. How can mature-node expansion matter without solving leading-edge AI compute?

## Going deeper

- Compare company risk-factor language before and after major export-control updates.
- Follow capacity announcements for both advanced nodes and mature nodes.
- When reading a policy rule, map it to the semiconductor pipeline: design, wafer fab, equipment, packaging, product shipment, service, or end use.
