# Semiconductor Pipeline Capstone Quiz

This quiz pulls together the first part of the semiconductor pipeline track. You will reason about ecosystem roles, wafer process flow, yield and capacity, lithography, packaging and HBM, roofline limits, export controls, and bottleneck ranking.

Some questions are conceptual. Others use small numbers so you can calculate by hand. For parametric questions, retaking the quiz may generate new values.

Aim for explanations, not memorization. A good semiconductor analyst can connect a technical fact to the layer of the pipeline it constrains.
