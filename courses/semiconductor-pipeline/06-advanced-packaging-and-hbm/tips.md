## How to read packaging news

When you see an announcement about advanced packaging capacity, ask:

1. What exact process is constrained: interposer, bridge, substrate, attach,
   test, thermal assembly, or final system integration?
2. Is the quoted number wafers, packages, HBM stacks, or finished accelerators?
3. Has the customer qualified the line, or is the capacity only planned?
4. Are HBM supply and substrate supply scaling at the same pace?
5. Does the package support the next product generation's larger die or higher
   HBM stack count?

## Going deeper

- Read AI accelerator teardowns with the package diagram open. The physical
  placement of HBM around the die often explains the product's bandwidth story.
- Compare a normal server CPU package with an HBM accelerator package. The
  difference in routing density is the point.
- Track whether a capacity expansion adds generic assembly output or the
  specific advanced package flow that high-end accelerators require.
