## Reading tips

- Do not memorize every hybridization case at once. Start with carbonyls
  (sp2), saturated carbons (sp3), and aromatic rings (sp2).
- If a group has a charge, first ask whether resonance spreads that charge.
- If a molecule feels confusing, mark polar bonds with small arrows toward O,
  N, S, or halogens.
- For protein work, remember that amides are special: the peptide bond is
  planar because of resonance.
