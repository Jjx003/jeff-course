## Reading tips

- Treat "hydrophobic" as a statement about water organization, not a magical
  repulsive force.
- A hydrogen bond has both chemistry and geometry. The donor and acceptor must
  be compatible and positioned well.
- When you see a charged group in a protein core, ask what compensates for
  desolvation.
- Amphipathic molecules are easier to reason about if you sketch polar heads
  and nonpolar tails separately.
