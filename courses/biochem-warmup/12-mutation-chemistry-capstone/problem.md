## Reason from chemistry to behavior

This capstone quiz asks you to reason from chemistry to protein behavior.

Treat each mutation as a small experiment. Ask:

- Is the residue buried or solvent exposed?
- Does the mutation add or remove charge?
- Does it alter hydrogen bonding, packing, or covalent stabilization?
- Does it change backbone flexibility?
- Could pH or local environment shift the expected protonation state?

These are the habits you will need before the protein-folding track.
