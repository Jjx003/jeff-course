## Functional group recognition

Functional groups are the reusable chemical patterns of biochemistry. Once you
can spot them, large molecules become easier to read: sugars are rich in
alcohols and carbonyl-derived chemistry, amino acids mix amines and carboxylic
acids, proteins contain amides, nucleotides carry phosphates, and many cofactors
use aromatic rings to move electrons or stack with other flat groups.
