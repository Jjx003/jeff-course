## Study tips

- When a diagram seems confusing, ask: "Which atoms point toward me, and which
  point away?"
- Do not infer R/S from L/D. Treat them as separate labels unless you have
  explicitly assigned priorities.
- Remember glycine: no chiral alpha carbon, high flexibility.
- Remember cysteine: L but usually R by CIP priority because sulfur ranks
  highly.

## Common pitfalls

- **Flat drawings hide stereochemistry.** A skeletal formula without wedges,
  dashes, or stated stereochemistry may be incomplete for biology.
- **Mirror image does not mean same function.** In a chiral protein pocket,
  enantiomers can behave like different molecules.
- **All stereoisomers are not enantiomers.** Molecules with multiple
  stereocenters can form diastereomers.
