## Quick pH-versus-pKa calls

Many biochemistry decisions start with a quick pH-versus-pKa comparison. This drill asks for charge states as integers.

Use `0`, `1`, or `-1` as appropriate. The prompts are generated, so repeat rounds should feel familiar but not memorized.

When the pH exactly equals the pKa, the group is half-ionized, so neither charge state dominates — answer `0` in that case.
