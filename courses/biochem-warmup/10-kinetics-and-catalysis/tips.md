## Check your intuition

- If a reaction has $\Delta G < 0$, is it necessarily fast?
- If an enzyme lowers the activation barrier, does it change the final ratio of products to reactants?
- Why might binding a substrate too tightly make an enzyme worse?

## Useful contrast

Thermodynamics is the map of favored destinations. Kinetics is the road network and traffic.

In protein folding, the same distinction appears as native-state stability versus folding accessibility. A low-energy native fold is not enough if the chain keeps falling into long-lived alternatives.
