# Biochem Warm-up Midterm Test

This checkpoint covers the first half of the warm-up track: bonding and molecular shape, functional groups, pKa and charge state, water and the hydrophobic effect, stereochemistry, and peptide backbone geometry.

Work from chemical principles rather than memorized labels. Pay attention to protonation state, polarity, hydrogen-bonding roles, and how three-dimensional geometry changes molecular recognition.

You will see a mix of conceptual questions and short calculation questions. Correctness and explanations are shown after you submit the full test.
