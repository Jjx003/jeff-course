# Resilience Scenario Modeling

Resilience planning should be quantitative enough to discipline arguments, even when uncertainty is high.

![Resilience scenario portfolio comparing probability, impact, and mitigations](/courses/supply-chain-systems/resilience-portfolio.svg)

Implement the missing functions in the starter code. You will compare disruption scenarios by expected annual loss and estimate the net value of mitigations such as dual sourcing, safety stock, and alternate logistics lanes.
