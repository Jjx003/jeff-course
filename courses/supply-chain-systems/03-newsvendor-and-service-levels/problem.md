# Newsvendor Logic and Service Levels

The newsvendor model is a compact way to reason about one-shot inventory decisions under uncertain demand. It appears in retail buys, spare-parts stocking, perishable goods, seasonal inventory, and capacity reservations.

![Newsvendor critical fractile on a demand distribution](/courses/supply-chain-systems/newsvendor-critical-fractile.svg)

Your goal is to learn why the right service level is not always "as high as possible." It depends on the cost of being short versus the cost of being long.
