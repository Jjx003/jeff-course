# Supply Chains as Coupled Systems

A supply chain is not just a procurement department or a shipping plan. It is a coupled operating system that turns uncertain demand into fulfilled orders through decisions about capacity, inventory, information, contracts, transportation, and recovery.

![Supply chain system map](/courses/supply-chain-systems/supply-chain-system-map.svg)

In this module you will build the vocabulary used throughout the course:

- nodes, arcs, buffers, and lead times
- decoupling points and order penetration points
- physical flow, information flow, financial flow, and risk flow
- local optimization versus system performance

By the end, you should be able to sketch a supply chain and explain where delays, variability, and incentives enter the system.
