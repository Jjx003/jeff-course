# Demand Forecasting and Forecast Error

Forecasts are not predictions to worship. They are decision inputs with measurable error.

![Forecast error funnel widening over the planning horizon](/courses/supply-chain-systems/forecast-error-funnel.svg)

This module focuses on the operational questions:

- What horizon does the decision require?
- Is the forecast biased?
- Is the demand process stable, seasonal, promotional, or intermittent?
- How does forecast error translate into stockouts, excess inventory, expediting, and lost trust?
