# Quiz: Partitions and Pentagons

This quiz covers the fundamental tools for counting integer partitions. We'll test your knowledge of:
- The structure of the partition generating function.
- The meaning of each term in the product expansion.
- Euler's Pentagonal Number Theorem and its associated recurrence.

### Prerequisites
- Knowledge of the partition function $p(n)$.
- Understanding of the generating function $P(x) = \prod_{k=1}^{\infty} (1 - x^k)^{-1}$.
- Familiarity with generalized pentagonal numbers $g_m = \frac{m(3m-1)}{2}$.
