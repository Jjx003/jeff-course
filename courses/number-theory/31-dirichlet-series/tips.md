## Summary of the Course

You have reached the end of the **Number Theory** track! We have traveled from the basic definitions of divisibility to the sophisticated heights of analytic number theory and $p$-adic analysis.

### Recap of the Bridge
The relationship between arithmetic functions and Dirichlet series is a microcosm of modern mathematics:
- **Algebra:** The structure of convolution and group theory.
- **Analysis:** Convergence of series and complex variables.
- **Arithmetic:** The prime numbers and the Fundamental Theorem.

### Going Deeper
To continue your journey, consider exploring these advanced topics:
1. **The Prime Number Theorem:** Use the properties of $\zeta(s)$ (specifically that it has no zeros on $\text{Re}(s)=1$) to prove $\pi(x) \sim x/\ln x$.
2. **Modular Forms:** Functions that transform under $SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$, linking number theory to geometry and physics.
3. **Algebraic Number Theory:** Generalizing these ideas to number fields other than $\mathbb{Q}$.
4. **The Riemann Hypothesis:** The most famous open problem in math, concerning the non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$.

### Recommended Reading
- *An Introduction to the Theory of Numbers* by G.H. Hardy and E.M. Wright.
- *A Course in Arithmetic* by Jean-Pierre Serre.
- *Multiplicative Number Theory* by Harold Davenport.
