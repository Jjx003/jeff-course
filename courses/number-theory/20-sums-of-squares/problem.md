One of the most classic applications of binary quadratic forms (and number theory in general) is determining which integers can be expressed as the sum of perfect squares.

- Which numbers are of the form $x^2 + y^2$?
- Which numbers are of the form $x^2 + y^2 + z^2$?
- Which numbers are of the form $w^2 + x^2 + y^2 + z^2$?

In this module, we will state and prove two crowning achievements of classical number theory: **Fermat's Theorem on Sums of Two Squares** and **Lagrange's Four-Square Theorem**.

As we will see, these questions are deeply intertwined with prime factorization, quadratic reciprocity, and the arithmetic of complex numbers (Gaussian integers) and quaternions.
