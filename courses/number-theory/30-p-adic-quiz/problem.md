# Quiz: $p$-adic Foundations

This quiz covers the fundamental properties of $p$-adic numbers. The goal is not just to compute a norm, but to internalize the reversal of ordinary size: high divisibility by $p$ means small $p$-adic distance.

You should be comfortable moving between the valuation $v_p(x)$, the norm $|x|_p$, and the geometric consequences of the ultrametric inequality.

This quiz covers:

- $p$-adic valuation $v_p(x)$
- $p$-adic norm $|x|_p$
- The Ultrametric Inequality
- Properties of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ and $\mathbb{Q}_p$

Make sure you are comfortable with the definition of $|x|_p = p^{-v_p(x)}$ and how the "non-Archimedean" nature of the norm affects convergence and geometry.
