# Divisibility and Proofs

Welcome to the Number Theory track! Number theory is one of the oldest and purest branches of mathematics, focused primarily on the properties of integers.

Before we can compute greatest common divisors or encrypt messages using prime numbers, we must build a rigorous foundation. In this first module, we will explore the fundamental concept of **divisibility**. 

We will introduce formal notation, state the Division Algorithm, and practice constructing rigorous mathematical proofs using block LaTeX formatting. 

## Learning Objectives

- Understand and apply the formal definition of divisibility ($a \mid b$).
- Prove basic properties of divisibility such as transitivity and linearity.
- Comprehend the statement and significance of the Division Algorithm.
- Learn how to structure a rigorous number theory proof.

When you are ready, continue to the **Theory** section to begin our exploration.
