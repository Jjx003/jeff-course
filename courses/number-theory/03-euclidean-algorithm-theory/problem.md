# The Euclidean Algorithm

One of the oldest and most important algorithms in mathematics was documented by Euclid around 300 BC in his *Elements*. The Euclidean Algorithm provides an incredibly efficient method to compute the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of two integers.

In this module, we will define the GCD formally and prove why the Euclidean Algorithm works using the principles of divisibility and the Division Algorithm we established in Module 01.

## Learning Objectives

- Formalize the definition of the Greatest Common Divisor.
- Understand the core lemma: $\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(b, a \bmod b)$.
- Prove that the Euclidean Algorithm correctly computes the GCD and always terminates.
- Trace the steps of the algorithm manually.

When you are ready, continue to the **Theory** section to dive into the elegant logic behind this ancient algorithm.
