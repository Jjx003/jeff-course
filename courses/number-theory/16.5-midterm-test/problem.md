# Midterm Test: Foundations Through Quadratic Residues

This test checks the first half of the number theory course, from divisibility and proof techniques through quadratic residues.

You should be comfortable with:

- Divisibility, gcds, Bezout identities, and Euclid's algorithm
- Prime factorization and consequences of the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic
- Modular arithmetic, congruence classes, and modular inverses
- Chinese remainder systems and compatibility conditions
- Hensel lifting in the nonsingular case
- Primitive roots and orders modulo primes
- Quadratic residues, Legendre symbols, and Euler's criterion

Work each question carefully. Correctness and explanations are shown only after you finish the test.
