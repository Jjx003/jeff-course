# Divisibility Knowledge Check

This quiz tests your grasp of the foundational concepts introduced in the previous module. Treat each question like a small proof obligation: identify the definition being used, translate the divisibility statement into an equation, and only then choose the answer.

You will answer questions about:

- The formal definition of $a \mid b$.
- How linear combinations preserve divisibility.
- What the Division Algorithm guarantees.
- How to read a proof from its hypotheses to its conclusion.

Remember, to pass the quiz and earn points, you must score at least **70%**. You can retake the quiz as many times as you like.
