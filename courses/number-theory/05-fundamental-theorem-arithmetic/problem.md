Welcome to the heart of multiplicative number theory! In the previous modules, you implemented the Euclidean algorithm and used it to find greatest common divisors efficiently. Now, we turn our attention to the building blocks of integers themselves: **prime numbers**.

In this module, you will explore:

1. The formal definition of prime and composite numbers.
2. Euclid's elegant proof that there are infinitely many primes.
3. The **Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic**, which states that every integer greater than 1 can be uniquely factored into primes.

Understanding unique factorization is crucial for our next step, where you'll implement algorithms to generate primes and factorize numbers in the subsequent coding module.

Let's dive into the theory and explore why primes are considered the "atoms" of arithmetic!
