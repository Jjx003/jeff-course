# Primes and Factorization Check

Now that you have studied the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic and Euclid's proofs, use this quiz to check both facts and proof structure.

Before starting, make sure you can explain why Euclid's Lemma is the bridge from "prime" to "unique factorization." Many wrong answers look plausible if you only remember the theorem statement but not the role of divisibility.

This quiz covers:

- Identifying primes and composite numbers.
- Understanding Euclid's Lemma and its implications.
- The mechanics behind the proof of infinitely many primes.
- The uniqueness claim in prime factorization.

Take your time and think carefully about each question.
