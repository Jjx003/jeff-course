# Quiz: Quadratic Residues

It's time to check your understanding of quadratic residues and Euler's Criterion. In the previous module, we learned that a number $a$ is a quadratic residue modulo an odd prime $p$ if $x^2 \equiv a \pmod p$ has a solution. We also introduced the **Legendre symbol** $\left(\frac{a}{p}\right)$ and proved **Euler's Criterion**.

This quiz will test:

1. Your ability to identify quadratic residues and nonresidues.
2. The multiplicative properties of the Legendre symbol.
3. The application of Euler's Criterion to compute Legendre symbols.

Take your time and consider the properties of squares in modular arithmetic!
