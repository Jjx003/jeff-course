Up to this point, we've focused on exact divisibility and prime factorizations. We now introduce one of the most powerful tools in all of number theory: **Modular Arithmetic** and the **Theory of Congruences**.

Introduced by Carl Friedrich Gauss in his seminal 1801 book *Disquisitiones Arithmeticae*, the notation of congruence formalizes the idea of "clock arithmetic." It provides a systematic way to work with remainders.

In this module, you will learn:
1. The formal definition of congruences.
2. How congruence relations behave exactly like equality for addition, subtraction, and multiplication.
3. The rules and caveats for division in modular arithmetic.

Understanding congruences is essential for everything that follows, including the Chinese Remainder Theorem and cryptographic algorithms!
