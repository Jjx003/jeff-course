# Final Test: Number Theory

This final test covers the whole number theory track. It emphasizes connections between proof techniques, modular computation, algebraic structure, and analytic or generating-function viewpoints.

Expect questions from:

- Divisibility, gcds, primes, and modular arithmetic
- Chinese remainders, Hensel lifting, primitive roots, and quadratic residues
- Quadratic reciprocity, Jacobi symbols, binary quadratic forms, and sums of squares
- Continued fractions and Pell equations
- Integer partitions and generating functions
- p-adic numbers, valuations, arithmetic functions, convolution, and Dirichlet series

Use scratch work where needed. Correctness and explanations are shown only after you submit the finished test.
