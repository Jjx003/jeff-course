# Quiz: Continued Fractions

You have learned how to compute continued fractions and how convergents provide unusually good rational approximations. This quiz checks whether the recurrence and the approximation story have connected.

As you work, keep track of the indexing convention. Many continued-fraction mistakes are not conceptual; they come from swapping a numerator recurrence with a denominator recurrence, or from forgetting which coefficient was just added.

This quiz covers:

- Identifying finite vs. infinite continued fractions.
- Applying the recurrence relations for convergents $p_n$ and $q_n$.
- Understanding the difference between successive convergents.
- Recognizing why quadratic irrationals lead to periodic continued fractions.
