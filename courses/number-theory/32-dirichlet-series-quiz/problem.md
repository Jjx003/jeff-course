# Final Quiz: Dirichlet Series

Congratulations on reaching the end of the course. This final quiz connects the algebra of arithmetic functions with the analytic language of Dirichlet series.

The main idea to keep in mind is that multiplication of Dirichlet series corresponds to Dirichlet convolution of coefficients. Euler products then appear when multiplicative functions let the prime-power data separate cleanly.

This final quiz tests your mastery of:

- Dirichlet convolution and its properties.
- Identifying multiplicative functions.
- The relationship between arithmetic functions and Dirichlet series.
- Euler products for multiplicative functions.

Read each question slowly: most of the work is recognizing which operation is happening.
