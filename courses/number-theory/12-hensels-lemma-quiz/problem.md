# Hensel's Lemma Quiz

In the previous module, we learned how to use Hensel's Lemma to "lift" roots of polynomials from modulo $p^k$ to modulo $p^{k+1}$. 
This quiz will test your understanding of the conditions required for Hensel's Lemma to apply, and how to compute the lifted roots.

You will need to use the lifting formula:
$$ t \equiv -\frac{f(r)}{p^k} \cdot [f'(r)]^{-1} \pmod p $$
where the lifted root is $s = r + t p^k$.

Take your time and use scratch paper if necessary!
