# Quiz: Sums of Squares

Now that we have explored Fermat's Two-Square Theorem and Lagrange's Four-Square Theorem, use this quiz to separate the similar-looking statements.

The key distinction is between existence theorems and classification theorems. Lagrange guarantees that every positive integer is a sum of four squares. Fermat's two-square theorem is more selective: the exponents of primes $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ matter.

This quiz covers:

- Which integers can be written as the sum of two squares.
- The implications of prime factorizations on sums of squares.
- Properties of Lagrange's Four-Square Theorem.

Keep the prime-factorization condition in view as you answer.
